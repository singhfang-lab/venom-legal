Venom Cam Support

Questions, bugs or ideas: email singh.fang@gmail.com. Tell us your iPhone model, iOS version and the app version (Settings in the app), and attach a screenshot if you can.

Subscriptions and purchases

  • Restore purchases: in the app, Settings → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought with.
  • Cancel or change a subscription: Settings → Manage Subscription in the app, or iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid the next charge.
  • Free trial: the yearly plan includes a 7-day free trial for new subscribers. If you cancel during the trial you are not charged.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Cameras only is a one-time purchase that unlocks all film cameras. It does not include AI refine.

Photos

  • Photos you take are kept in the app on your iPhone. Save the ones you want to your photo library; deleting the app deletes photos that were not saved.
  • AI refine sends a copy of the chosen photo to our processing service; the original is never changed. See the privacy policy.

Venom Cam 支持

问题、故障或建议，请发邮件到 singh.fang@gmail.com。请写上 iPhone 型号、iOS 版本和 App 版本（App 内设置可见），能附截图更好。

订阅与购买

  • 恢复购买：App 内 设置 → 恢复购买，需登录购买时的 Apple ID。
  • 取消或更改订阅：App 内 设置 → 管理订阅，或 iPhone 设置 → 你的名字 → 订阅。请在续费前至少 24 小时取消，以免下期扣费。
  • 免费试用：年付方案为新订阅者提供 7 天免费试用，试用期内取消不收费。
  • 退款由 Apple 处理：reportaproblem.apple.com。
  • 仅解锁相机是一次性购买，解锁全部胶片相机，不含 AI 精修。

照片

  • 拍摄的照片保存在 iPhone 上的 App 内。想保留的请存入照片图库；删除 App 会删除未保存的照片。
  • AI 精修会把所选照片的副本发送到处理服务，原片不会被改动。详见隐私政策。
