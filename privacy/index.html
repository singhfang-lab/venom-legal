Venom Cam Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026

Venom Cam (“the app”, “we”) is a camera and photo app. Your photos stay on your iPhone unless you choose a feature that needs a server. This policy explains what leaves your device, why, and what you can do about it.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Photos and videos you take or import, your edits, looks and albums are stored in the app on your device. We have no copy of them.
  • Location. If you turn on “Record location”, the place a photo was taken is written into that photo on your device. It is never sent to us or to the AI service.
  • Photo library. With your permission the app reads photos you choose to import and saves photos you choose to export. It does not upload your library.
  • Camera and microphone are used only to take photos and record Live Photo sound.

What is sent to a server, and only when you use it

FeatureWhat is sent
AI refine (including “refine again”)A re-encoded copy of the photo you chose, with EXIF data and location removed; the style you picked and any note you type. For Live Photos you choose to recolour, the Live Photo video.
Photo descriptionA small, reduced copy of the photo (no EXIF or location).
Daily storiesSmall thumbnails of that day’s photos, their capture times, and any instruction you type.
All of the aboveA random device identifier created by the app (not your Apple ID, name, email or advertising identifier), and, if you allow notifications, a push token so we can tell you when a result is ready.

These features are processed by our AI processing provider, which runs the image service and the AI models that produce results. The service keeps results and task records for a limited time (currently about 24 hours) so the app can download them; the finished result is then stored on your device. We use this data only to provide the feature you asked for. We do not sell it, and we do not use it for advertising.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. The app reads Apple-signed purchase records on your device to unlock what you bought. Each purchase carries a random account identifier generated by the app, so that we can later recognise your purchases across devices without knowing who you are.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, no tracking across other apps or websites, no data brokers.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No account with your name or email is required.

Your choices

  • Don’t use AI refine, descriptions or stories, and nothing about your photos leaves your iPhone.
  • Turn off location, photo, camera, microphone or notification access at any time in iOS Settings.
  • Deleting the app deletes the photos and data it stores on your device (save anything you want to keep to your library first).
  • To ask for deletion of server-side data linked to your device identifier, or any privacy question, contact us at the address below.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If we change what is collected — for example when we add accounts or new AI features — we will update this page and the date above before the change takes effect.

Venom Cam 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 3 日

Venom Cam（下称“本 App”“我们”）是一款相机与照片应用。除非你主动使用需要服务器的功能，你的照片只保存在你的 iPhone 上。本政策说明哪些内容会离开你的设备、原因，以及你能做什么。

只留在你 iPhone 上的

  • 你拍摄或导入的照片和视频、编辑、影调和相册，都保存在设备上的本 App 内，我们没有副本。
  • 位置。开启“记录位置”后，拍摄地点只写入设备上的这张照片，不会发给我们或 AI 服务。
  • 照片图库。经你允许，本 App 读取你选择导入的照片、保存你选择导出的照片，不会上传你的图库。
  • 相机和麦克风只用于拍照和录制实况照片的声音。

只有在你使用时才会发送到服务器的

功能发送的内容
AI 精修（含“再修一次”）你所选照片的重新编码副本（已去除 EXIF 与位置）、你选的风格和填写的备注；你选择为实况照片调色时，还有实况视频。
照片解读照片的缩小副本（无 EXIF、无位置）。
每日故事当天照片的小缩略图、拍摄时间，以及你填写的要求。
以上全部本 App 生成的随机设备标识（不是 Apple ID、姓名、邮箱或广告标识符）；如你允许通知，还有推送令牌，用于结果完成时提醒你。

这些功能由我们的 AI 处理服务商处理，它运行图像服务及生成结果的 AI 模型。服务端为了让 App 取回结果，会短期保留结果和任务记录（目前约 24 小时），成片随后保存在你的设备上。这些数据只用于提供你所请求的功能，我们不出售，也不用于广告。

购买

订阅和购买由 Apple 处理，我们收不到你的支付信息。本 App 在设备上读取 Apple 签名的购买记录来解锁你买的内容。每次购买会附带本 App 生成的随机账户标识，便于以后在你的多台设备间识别购买，而不需要知道你是谁。

我们不做的事

  • 没有广告，不跨 App 或网站追踪，不向数据经纪商提供数据。
  • 没有接入统计分析或崩溃上报 SDK。
  • 不需要用姓名或邮箱注册账号。

你的选择

  • 不使用 AI 精修、解读或故事，你的照片就不会离开 iPhone。
  • 可随时在 iOS 设置中关闭位置、照片、相机、麦克风或通知权限。
  • 删除本 App 会删除它保存在设备上的照片和数据（想保留的请先存入图库）。
  • 如需删除与你设备标识相关的服务端数据，或有任何隐私问题，请通过下方地址联系我们。

儿童

本 App 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，我们不会有意收集其个人信息。

变更

如果收集的内容发生变化（例如加入账号或新的 AI 功能），我们会在变更生效前更新本页及上方日期。
